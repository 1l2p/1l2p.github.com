[Andreas Meiszner](832http://www.linkedin.com/pub/1/001/b88) gave an interesting presentation at the 4th International Conference on Higher Education, where he highlighted the power of open source processes for education, and how that could pose a threat to the way universities are organising teaching and learning today. He ended with a great quote:

> “In the future, universities will no be competing with other universities only, but they will compete with the Web. And you can’t compete with the Web.” 

I spoke just after Andreas and it was great to use his very practical examples to support my broader theoretical argument that looking at the universities’ position towards governance of knowledge — open or closed — is a good starting point to redefine the social role of the institution. 

Both papers will be available from the [GUNI website](http://www.guni-rmies.net/k2008/workshops.php?lang=2&amp;id=35&amp;lin_t=2) shortly.
