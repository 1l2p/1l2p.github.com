This must be one of the greatest places to work in the field of open education in the world. The Center for Open and Sustainable Learning COSL at Utah State University has a faculty opening for Academics working in the field of technology and learning. I have met Steve and his colleagues at numerous occasions and these guys have continuously been pushing the boundaries of how we think about education, and what technology we use to make it happen. And they are fun to hang out with!

More [here](http://blog.opencontent.org/2008/01/15/come-work-at-usu/), or go straight to the [job description](http://jobs.usu.edu/applicants/Central?quickFind=52908). Good luck!
