I was happy to see [Brandon Muramatsu](http://www.mura.org/) at the OCWC Vietnam meeting and get some time to hang out, check out the latest gadges (geek!) and go to his presentation. He asked the audience – “what would you like to be able to do with MIT OCW?” Turns out he and [Vijay Kumar](http://mit.edu/vkumar/www/) (who is on the [P2PU advisory group](http://p2pu.org/advisors)) are plotting something called [Greenfield](http://greenfield.mit.edu/) that would bolt a few useful services onto a copy of the MIT OCW archive. Very exciting for P2PU and I tracked him down after his presentation for a short chat about the project.

He was nice enough to offer his digital camera to record – and, voila, video blog number 2. As I am trying to better understand the value of video (thanks [Stian and John for comments](http://bokaap.net/open-edu/thoughts-on-disrupting-class-and-leadership-without-easy-answers/#comments)) this conversation approach definitely makes a lot more sense to me.
