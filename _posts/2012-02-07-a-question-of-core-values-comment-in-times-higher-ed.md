A couple of weeks ago I wrote a [post on Apple’s move into the textbook world](/2012/01/20/apple-edu-its-a-revolution-just-not-ours.html). It got quite a lot of attention and I wrote a slightly longer version for publication in the Times Higher Ed. I managed to add a little context from other initiatives in the field, including MITx. The full THE piece is [here](http://www.timeshighereducation.co.uk/story.asp?sectioncode=26&storycode=418902&c=1) and a brief excerpt below:

> Apple’s vision is a walled garden that offers a carefully curated experience to those willing to lock themselves into it. It will be shiny and beautiful, but education will be a commodity and Apple the company through which we will consume it.

> MIT’s vision is bolder. It sets us on the course to an educational future in which anyone, regardless of background, budget or location, has access to a high-quality education – even those who don’t own iPads.
