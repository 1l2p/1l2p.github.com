[Dick Ngambi](http://www.cet.uct.ac.za/DickNgambi) invited me to speak with [Masters students](http://www.cet.uct.ac.za/masters) at University of Cape Town as part of his course on “Educational ICTs for Developing Contexts”. I am chuffed to be invited back – last year we had a very lively discussion on creating an African version of Michael Wesch’s [youtube sensation](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGCJ46vyR9o). This year I will be speaking about “OER beyond content” and especially Peer 2 Peer University and its relevance and opportunities for developing countries.

Dick asked me for a few readings to distribute in advance. I sent him the following, but would love suggestions on what to add to the list.

- **Openness, Dynamic Specialization, and the Disaggregated Future of Higher Education**

David Wiley and John Hilton III

[http://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/768/1414](http://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/768/1414)
- [](http://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/768/1414)**Breaking Higher Education’s Iron Triangle: Access, Cost, and Quality**

John Daniel, Asha Kanwar, and Stamenka Uvalic-Trumbic

[http://www.changemag.org/Archives/Back%20Issues/March-April%202009/full-iron-triangle.html](http://www.changemag.org/Archives/Back%20Issues/March-April%202009/full-iron-triangle.html)
- [](http://www.changemag.org/Archives/Back%20Issues/March-April%202009/full-iron-triangle.html)**Minds on Fire: Open Education, the Long Tail, and Learning 2.0**

John Seely Brown and Richard P. Adler

EDUCAUSE Review, vol. 43, no. 1 (January/February 2008): 16–32
- **Short video intro to P2PU**

[http://blogs.p2pu.org/blog/2010/04/29/p2pu-the-speakeasy-of-open-education/](http://blogs.p2pu.org/blog/2010/04/29/p2pu-the-speakeasy-of-open-education/)
- [](http://blogs.p2pu.org/blog/2010/04/29/p2pu-the-speakeasy-of-open-education/)If students have access to the early chapters of the following book, I would also recommend:

**Disrupting Class**

Clayton M Christensen, Michael B. Horn and Curtis W. Johnson
