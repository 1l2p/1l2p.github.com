Cheryl Hodgkinson-Williams from UCT’s [Opening Scholarship](http://blogs.uct.ac.za/blog/openingscholarship) project and I ran a short OER Workshop for participants of the [ICEL 2008](http://www.academic-conferences.org/icel/icel2008/icel08-home.htm) conference yesterday. We split the workshop into a shorter seminar/presentation and a longer hands-on practical session and ended up having a lot of fun with participants from the Cape Town universities as well as from other South African institutions, and people from Namibia and Ethiopia.

The [workshop wiki](http://free.uwc.ac.za/sandbox/index.php/ICEL_OER_Workshop) is online and we would love to get feedback and comments for improvement. Some participants already asked us to run the event in their universities and we are planning to build a workshop blueprint/model that others can use as well.
