Previously classified images from the [iSummit 2008](http://icommonssummit.org/) show [David Wiley](http://opencontent.org/blog/) signing the [Cape Town Open Education Declaration](http://www.capetowndeclaration.org/) through a ground-breaking interpretive dance display. The sequence starts with Dr Wiley dressed in a [Utah Open High School](http://openhighschool.org/) T-Shirt, which he gradually combines with the “I signed the Cape Town Declaration” shirt that was released during the iSummit. Live remix culture … bravo Dr Wiley!
